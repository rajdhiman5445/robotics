## Purpose

Use this template when you want a page to survive long after the course or project context is gone.

<Callout type="note" title="Rule of thumb">
  Explain the idea once, then link to the permanent concept instead of repeating it everywhere.
</Callout>

## Recommended structure

1. Why it matters
2. Core objects or definitions
3. Example
4. Common failure modes
5. Further reading
